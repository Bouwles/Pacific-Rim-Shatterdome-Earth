# Third-party assets

Every imported file, where it came from, its licence, what was changed, and
where it lives. Nothing here is ripped from a game or a film. Everything is
under a licence that allows use in this project; attribution is given in the
in-game credits as well, whether or not the licence requires it.

## People (CC0 1.0, Quaternius, via poly.pizza)

Animated low-poly humans by Quaternius, published under Creative Commons Zero
1.0 (public domain dedication). Downloaded as GLB from poly.pizza's direct
links. Modification: none to the files; at load the game normalises each
model's height, sets PBR metallic to 0 and roughness to 0.85, and tints
named cloth materials with a role colour. Destination: `public/assets/models/people/`.

Kenney (www.kenney.nl) publishes these under Creative Commons Zero. Downloaded
as the official zips from kenney.nl; only the GLB models were kept. No
modification to the files. Attribution "Kenney" in credits, by choice.

## Not imported

No model, texture, recording or music from any Pacific Rim film or game. No
Mixamo animation (its licence needs an Adobe account). No asset requiring an
account, key or payment. Music and the score remain original and synthesised.
